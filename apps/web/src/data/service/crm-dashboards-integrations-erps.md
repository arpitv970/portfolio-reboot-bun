## Automate Your Business Processes

Businesses often waste time on manual tasks, enter the same data in multiple places, and struggle to get clear insights from scattered information. I help automate your workflows and connect your systems so you can focus on growing your business.

## What I Build

### Custom Dashboards

Turn your data into useful visualizations that show:

- Sales performance and pipeline status in real time
- Customer behavior patterns and value insights
- Key business metrics that matter to your bottom line
- Simple forecasts based on your historical data

### System Integrations

Connect your different software so they talk to each other automatically. I can work with:

- Marketing automation platforms and email marketing tools
- Customer relationship management and sales software
- Help desk and customer support systems
- Accounting software and payment processors
- E-commerce platforms and online stores

Every business uses different tools, so I focus on understanding your specific software stack and creating the connections you need.

### Business Operations

Handle your core business processes with custom solutions:

- Financial management and automated invoicing
- Inventory tracking and stock management
- Employee and contractor management
- Supply chain and vendor coordination

## How It Works

I connect your different software systems so data flows automatically between them. Your customer information, sales data, and operational metrics end up in one dashboard where you can see everything at once.
